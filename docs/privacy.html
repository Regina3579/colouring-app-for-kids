Privacy Policy

App: Coloring Fun · Last updated: June 29, 2026

Coloring Fun (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a coloring and drawing app for children. We care deeply about your family’s privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. We do not collect personal information

Coloring Fun does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information about you or your child. There are no accounts, no logins, and no servers that receive your data.

2. No tracking or advertising

The app contains no third-party advertising, no analytics, and no tracking technologies.

3. Your drawings stay on your device

Pictures you color and drawings you create are stored locally on your device. If you choose “Save to Photos,” the image is saved to your device’s photo library with your permission. These images are never uploaded to us or any third party.

4. Photo library permission

The app requests permission to add images to your Photos only when you tap Save to Photos. It does not read or access your existing photos.

5. Purchases

Coloring Fun offers an optional “Pro” auto-renewable subscription. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID account settings.

6. Children’s privacy

Coloring Fun is designed for children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else. We comply with applicable children’s privacy laws, including COPPA and GDPR-K. Because we collect no data, there is nothing to disclose, sell, or delete.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
